# Minigame cover artwork

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool (one generation per asset, no CLI/API fallback). These are decorative arcade illustrations, not club photographs. They contain no baked-in titles, prices, sponsor logos or match data; Vue renders all labels and rewards.

Production files are 320 × 320 WebP, exported mechanically with Pillow/Lanczos at quality 82, method 6. Originals remain in the built-in generation folder; all runtime assets are stored here in the project. No generation service is called by the app.

## Final prompt set

Each final prompt is the following shared specification followed by its corresponding subject below.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: small mobile sports minigame card background, square raster illustration. Style: polished playful 3D arcade game art, chunky sculpted forms, rich color, glossy highlights, cinematic rim light, simple bold silhouette readable at thumbnail size. Composition: main subject centered in upper half, fully visible with generous margin at sides, quieter dark background across lower third where the app will overlay its title. One cohesive scene, full bleed. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, borders or UI. 

### mvp.webp

A shining gold basketball MVP trophy with a small basketball crowning its cup, dramatic amber spotlights and a few floating gold confetti pieces, deep burgundy arena background. Celebratory and premium.

### reaction.webp

A luminous electric yellow lightning bolt striking a round arcade reaction target, energetic electric sparks and motion trails, deep violet background. Exciting and fast.

### quiz.webp

A friendly glossy coral-pink sculpted brain hovering above three glowing blue answer tiles, a few little stars, deep indigo background. Clever and playful.

### basketball.webp

An orange basketball arcing into a bright red hoop with a white net, dynamic golden motion trail, stylized indoor arena in a deep blue background. Dynamic close-up sports action.

### playmaker.webp

Two expressive stylized athletic hands making a crisp basketball pass, one releasing and another receiving a bright orange ball along a curved glowing mint motion trail, deep teal indoor court background. Clever teamwork, no faces or full figures.

### crossover.webp

An orange basketball zipping low between two chunky basketball sneakers, one coral red shoe and one cream white shoe, exaggerated zigzag violet motion trails on a deep purple basketball court. Quick crossover dribble, no full person.

### tap.webp

A stylized hand with an extended index finger tapping a large luminous coral red arcade button, concentric golden shockwave rings and a few sparks, deep cobalt blue background. Tactile, fast and satisfying.

### sponsor-rush.webp

Several colorful blank glossy ticket-shaped tokens flying in an upward spiral with a gold coin and bright motion trails, deep magenta arcade background. Tokens have completely blank centers, no symbols, logos, letters or text. Energetic catching-game scene.

### memory-flash.webp
